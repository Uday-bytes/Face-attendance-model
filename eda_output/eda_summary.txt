6. Findings and what they mean for the project
----------------------------------------------
1. Only 7% of people have >= 5 images, and 71% have just one. Same-person comparisons need several images, so use --min-images 5 for LFW, and collect 5-10 images per consenting student for your own data.
2. 11% of images are very dark or very bright. Lighting varies a lot, so test the app under bright, normal and dim light and consider brightness normalisation (for example CLAHE) before detection.
3. 14% of images look blurry (low Laplacian variance). Ask students to register with a steady, in-focus photo.
4. Detection succeeds on 99.5% of images, so failed detections are a minor issue here.
5. 14% of images contain several faces. Registration in the app needs exactly one face, so those images would be rejected.
6. The equal-error threshold on this data is 0.17, which differs from the app's 0.45. Choose the threshold by weighing FAR against FRR (plot 13).

Note: heuristic limits (dark/bright/blurry/small-face) are starting points; adjust them in the settings at the top of EDA.py after looking at the histograms.
